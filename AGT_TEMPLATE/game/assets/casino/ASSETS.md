# Placeholder asset provenance

The models, PNG textures and WAV files in this directory were generated from
scratch with Python during AI-assisted development. They
were not downloaded from an asset marketplace. No third-party asset licence
is claimed for these original generated outputs.

- `models/chair.obj`, `lamp.obj`, `planter.obj`: distinct arrangements of cuboids,
  exported as triangulated OBJ with normals, UVs and MTL diffuse texture paths.
- `textures/`: small procedural RGB PNGs; sign lettering uses hand-specified 5x7
  bitmap patterns. The six sky images are identical solid colours.
- `audio/`: original synthesised sine-wave cues and a quiet four-note background
  loop, exported as mono PCM16 WAV at 22050 Hz.

The inherited Arial font, university engine and bundled libraries are not
original casino assets. Their existing notices and licences still apply; see
`AGT_TEMPLATE/LICENSE.MD` and `AGT_TEMPLATE/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.
